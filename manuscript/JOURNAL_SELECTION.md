# Journal selection

## Decision

**Selected target:** *International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation* (IJAEO), article type **Research paper**.

**Fallback 1:** *IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing* (JSTARS).

**Fallback 2:** *Remote Sensing of Environment* (RSE).

This order follows the declared weighted score below. It is a fit decision, not a prediction of acceptance. No journal's scope excludes the article.

## Official-source screening

**Retrieved 23 September 2026.** Requirements can change; they must be rechecked immediately before portal submission.

### International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation

- **Scope and article type:** Original work applying Earth observation to natural resources and environmental questions is central. The scope explicitly includes land as a foundation for infrastructure and housing, natural hazards, data quality, spatial uncertainty, visualization, and interpretation. Research papers are an accepted article type.
- **Length and format:** A research paper may contain 8,000 words in the complete submission, including abstract, notes, tables, references, and appendices. The abstract is limited to 250 words; 1–7 keywords are required; numbered sections are requested. No numeric figure cap is stated. Artwork must be cited, separately supplied, sequentially numbered, and captioned.
- **Access model:** The journal is fully open access. Its submission checklist states that an article publishing charge applies on acceptance, subject to institutional or funder coverage; the contextual charge or waiver must be checked by the corresponding author.
- **Data/code:** Elsevier Option C applies: research data must be deposited, cited, and linked, or the paper must explain why sharing is not possible. A data-availability statement is required at submission.
- **AI policy:** Substantive generative-AI use must be declared in a dedicated section before the references, naming the tool and purpose and affirming human review and responsibility.
- **Submission components:** Editable manuscript source, title-page identities and affiliations, corresponding-author details, 3–5 highlights of at most 85 characters each, figures and captions, supplement, declarations, and CRediT roles.
- **Official sources:** [Guide for authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-applied-earth-observation-and-geoinformation/publish/guide-for-authors); [journal homepage and access model](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-applied-earth-observation-and-geoinformation).

**Assessment:** This is the strongest fit because the paper's contribution is an applied Earth-observation inference design built around data quality, spatial uncertainty, independent geometry, and explicit environmental-cause limits. The present length and eight-figure architecture fit without deleting a finding.

### IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing

- **Scope and article type:** JSTARS publishes new and significant applied remote-sensing and Earth-observation work, including integration with in-situ sensing, modeling, and information creation. The paper's independent-geometry and reproducibility design fits directly.
- **Length and format:** The journal-specific page does not state a general article page limit. It requires IEEE double-column, single-spaced format. Abstracts must be 150–250 words and self-contained. Figure files have IEEE-specific formats and resolution requirements; biographies and author photographs are requested.
- **Access model:** Fully open access under CC BY 4.0. The official page lists an APC of US$1,800 for manuscripts submitted on or after 1 January 2025.
- **Data/code:** IEEE encourages archiving and sharing associated data and code; the journal page emphasizes complete experimental data and sufficient methodological description.
- **AI policy:** IEEE requires AI-generated content to be disclosed in the acknowledgments, identifying the system, affected sections, and level of use. Disclosure for grammar-only editing is recommended but generally outside the mandatory policy.
- **Submission components:** IEEE-formatted manuscript, IEEE references, numbered pages, graphics, ORCID for every author, author biographies and photographs, and complete corresponding-author contact details.
- **Official sources:** [JSTARS information for authors](https://www.grss-ieee.org/publications/jstars-information-for-authors/); [IEEE submission and AI policies](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-and-peer-review-policies/); [IEEE data and code tools](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/authoring-tools-and-templates/tools-for-ieee-authors/).

**Assessment:** Strong topical and methodological fit, but conversion to IEEE layout/reference style, mandatory ORCIDs, biographies/photos, restricted graphics formats, and the fixed APC make it a less compatible immediate target.

### Remote Sensing of Environment

- **Scope and article type:** RSE covers remote-sensing science and applications, including urban and geological applications, subsidence, satellite time-series analysis, and change detection. Original Research Articles must advance remote-sensing science or application, not merely use remote sensing to investigate a local problem.
- **Length and format:** Research articles are limited to 15,000 words including references and figure captions. Abstracts are limited to 250 words; 1–7 keywords and 3–5 highlights are required. No numeric figure cap is stated.
- **Access model:** The journal supports open access and also provides a subscription route; license and any optional APC are selected after acceptance.
- **Data/code:** Elsevier Option B applies: deposit, citation, and linking are encouraged rather than mandatory, and a data statement is encouraged.
- **AI policy:** The same Elsevier declaration is required for substantive generative-AI use, in a dedicated section before the references.
- **Submission components:** Editable source, title page, highlights, figures and captions, supplement, declarations, and CRediT roles.
- **Official sources:** [Guide for authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/remote-sensing-of-environment/publish/guide-for-authors); [journal homepage and access model](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/remote-sensing-of-environment).

**Assessment:** The package fits the length and topic, but RSE asks for broad international significance and a statistically sound accuracy assessment or validation for every research paper. The current study intentionally stops at selective cross-geometry support because no suitable in-area ground reference exists. That is scientifically defensible, but it creates a higher editorial-fit risk than IJAEO or JSTARS.

## Adaptation decision

The IJAEO version keeps all eight main figures and three main tables because the official guide provides no lower numeric cap. The title avoids an unexplained abbreviation, the abstract is below 250 words, six keywords and five compliant highlights are supplied, numbered headings are retained, and the data/code and generative-AI declaration locations are defined. No scientific value, evidence state, negative control, or contradiction was changed for journal fit.
