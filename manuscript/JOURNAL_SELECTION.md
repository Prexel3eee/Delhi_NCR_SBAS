# Journal selection

## Decision

**Selected target:** *ISPRS Open Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing* (research article).

The corresponding author requested an ISPRS publication venue and left the exact journal to be fixed. This paper is an application-oriented Sentinel-1 InSAR study with a reproducibility and inference contribution, rather than a new general algorithm. The selected journal is an official ISPRS open-access journal that publishes scientific and technical work as well as application-oriented reports. That scope is the closest match among the society's named journals.

**Alternative if the authors seek a more selective methodological venue:** *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*. Its official scope includes SAR interferometry, image time-series analysis, urban modelling, and applications, but it emphasizes significant contributions of broad scientific interest. The current paper's evidence is from one metropolitan region, and its strongest result is deliberately bounded. A submission there would need an explicit case for broad transfer beyond Delhi-NCR.

**Alternative if the authors want a geo-information emphasis:** *ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information*. Its scope is geo-information science and application, whereas the present paper's primary measurement is radar interferometry.
