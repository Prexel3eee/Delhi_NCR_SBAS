# Verified paper cards

Metadata were checked against DOI registry records on 23 September 2026. Five papers supplied by the project owner were also inspected as full-text PDFs. A paper's mechanism interpretation is recorded separately from what it can support in this manuscript.

### P001 - Garg et al. (2022)

- **Verified citation:** Garg, S., Motagh, M., Indu, J. & Karanam, V. *Scientific Reports* 12, 651. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-021-04193-9.
- **Question:** Where and how rapidly did Delhi-NCR deform, and how did the patterns relate to groundwater decline?
- **Region/period:** Delhi-NCR; 2014-2020.
- **Sensor/geometry/method:** Sentinel-1 ascending and descending observations; PSI time-series analysis.
- **Validation:** Cross-geometry comparison and spatial/temporal comparison with groundwater records.
- **Numerical result:** Rates exceeding 11 cm yr-1 are reported at Kapashera; other reported zones include Faridabad and Dwarka.
- **Mechanism claim and support:** The authors associate subsidence with groundwater depletion using spatial correspondence and temporal trend comparison.
- **Limitations:** Observational association does not locate the compacting interval or exclude alternative causes; their identified regions are not interchangeable with the present frozen polygons.
- **Manuscript relevance:** Direct regional precedent; motivates independent geometry and explicit mechanism falsification.
- **Claims this paper may support:** Prior Delhi-NCR work reported rapid deformation and interpreted groundwater association.
- **Claims this paper may not support:** That H001/H004 in the present analysis are groundwater-driven; that relative LOS equals vertical subsidence.
- **Exact locator:** Abstract; Results subsections describing Kapashera, Faridabad and Dwarka; Discussion. Full-text PDF `paper 1 .pdf` inspected.
