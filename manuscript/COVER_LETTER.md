Please consider our **Research paper**, “Selective reproduction of localized line-of-sight deformation in Delhi-NCR using independent Sentinel-1 geometries,” for publication in the *International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation*.

The paper asks which localized urban deformation features defined in one Sentinel-1 geometry survive an independently assembled second geometry, and whether preregistered environmental proxies distinguish the surviving zones from credible negative controls. Two of five frozen zones, H001 and H004, reproduce at the spatial and mean-rate level. H002 and H003 do not reproduce despite adequate descending quality, and H005 remains an unresolved sign-and-magnitude contradiction. The manuscript preserves all three outcomes and separates observed relative line-of-sight support from vertical-motion and causal claims.

The contribution fits the journal's emphasis on applied Earth observation, geoinformation interpretation, data quality, and spatial uncertainty. It provides an auditable design that freezes detections before independent evaluation, retains negative and contradictory outcomes, and applies mechanism tests only after cross-geometry classification. The result is a reproducibility-centered approach to environmental inference rather than a stronger causal claim than the observations permit.
