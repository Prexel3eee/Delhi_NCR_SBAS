# Cover letter — ISPRS Open Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing

**Date:** 23 September 2026 (update on submission)
**To:** Editor-in-Chief, *ISPRS Open Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*

Dear Editor,

Please consider our **research article**, “Selective reproduction of localized line-of-sight deformation in Delhi-NCR using independent Sentinel-1 geometries,” for publication in the *ISPRS Open Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*.

The paper asks which localized urban deformation features defined in one Sentinel-1 geometry survive an independently assembled second geometry, and whether preregistered environmental proxies distinguish the surviving zones from credible negative controls. Two of five frozen zones, H001 and H004, reproduce at the spatial and mean-rate level. H002 and H003 do not reproduce despite adequate descending quality, and H005 remains an unresolved sign-and-magnitude contradiction. The manuscript preserves all three outcomes and separates observed relative line-of-sight support from vertical-motion and causal claims.

The paper contributes an auditable applied remote sensing design: detections are fixed before the second measurement, negative and contradictory outcomes remain visible, and environmental explanations are tested only after cross-geometry classification. These features align with the journal's scientific, technical, and application-oriented ISPRS remit.

The submission package includes the manuscript, supplement, eight main figures, four supplementary figures, three main tables, a claim-evidence ledger, figure provenance, and a machine-readable submission audit. Data and code locations are documented in the manuscript and Supplementary Section S13. The datasets generated or analysed for this study are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request; public remote sensing inputs are available from their providers. No dedicated dataset repository DOI is currently available. The authors will verify the journal's data policy and clarify any limits on sharing before submission.

**Exclusive submission:** The corresponding author confirms that the manuscript is original, has not been published previously, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors for submission.
